Questions

Gift questions

What if they already have picks?

They almost certainly do. They don’t have wooden ones. That’s why the Variety Pack is our most popular gift: eleven picks, six woods, one of everything we make.

I don’t know what they play. What’s safest?

The Variety Pack covers guitar, jazz, ukulele and bass. If it’s definitely guitar, the Guitar Sample Pack gives them five woods to try.

Can it come in a gift box?

Yes. Add the Tree Picks gift box for $4.99 and it arrives ready to give.

How fast does it ship?

Within 1–2 business days from Calgary. Use the delivery checker above for how long it takes to reach them.
