Questions

About the Variety Pack

What’s in the box?

Eleven picks. Guitar: maple, cherry, walnut, purpleheart and zebrawood. Jazz: maple, purpleheart and zebrawood. Ukulele: maple and cherry. Bass: ebony.

Will they arrive in time for Christmas?

Orders leave Calgary within 1–2 business days. Free shipping takes about 3–4 business days in Alberta and 5–7 to most of Canada. Paid shipping adds tracking.

Does it come gift-wrapped?

Add the Tree Picks gift box for $4.99 and it arrives ready to give.

Is it for acoustic or electric?

Both. That’s the point of the pack: try every wood on whatever you play and find the one you like best.
