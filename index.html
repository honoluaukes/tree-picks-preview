Questions

Good to know

Why use a pick made of wood?

In one word: tone. Wooden picks give a warmer sound and a different attack than plastic, and each wood sounds slightly different. They also compost much faster than plastic, and every pack plants a tree. But we’re musicians, and the main reason we use them is how they sound.

How fast does it ship, and is it really free?

Orders ship from Calgary within 1–2 business days. Shipping is free across Canada by regular mail (no tracking): about 1–2 days in Calgary, 3–4 in Alberta, 5–6 to BC through Quebec, 6–7 to Atlantic Canada. Paid options include tracking. US orders are a flat $7 USD and free over $40 USD.

Are they for acoustic or electric guitar?

Both. Softer woods like cherry suit a lighter touch and nylon strings; harder woods like purpleheart suit heavy strumming and rock. There are also picks made for bass, jazz and ukulele.

Will the picks break?

They’re made from solid hardwood and built to be played hard. One bass player put it this way: an hour on thick bass strings, and not a scratch.

Do you really plant a tree for every pack?

Yes. We don’t plant them ourselves, but a tree gets planted for every pack you order, through our Canadian partner The Good API.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes. Add a Tree Picks gift box for $4.99 and it arrives ready to give.
